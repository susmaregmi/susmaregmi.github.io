About Me

I am a graduate researcher with a background in Agricultural Science and a growing specialization in Geographic Information Systems (GIS), UAV remote sensing, spatial analysis, and precision agriculture.

I completed my B.Sc. in Agriculture from Agriculture and Forestry University, Nepal, where my academic and research work developed my interest in using geospatial technologies to understand agricultural landscapes and support data-driven agricultural decision-making.

I am currently pursuing a Master's degree in Environmental Science with a concentration in GIS at Tennessee State University. My current research focuses on the application of UAV imagery and spatial data for precision management of woody crops. My broader goal is to develop practical geospatial and remote-sensing approaches that connect agricultural data with real-world management decisions.

Research Focus & Interests

My current research centers on using UAV imagery and geospatial techniques to detect and map individual trees in agricultural landscapes, particularly fruit orchards. I am interested in how remote sensing, GIS, and computational methods can be integrated into practical agricultural workflows.

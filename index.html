About Me

My work sits at the intersection of agriculture, geospatial science, and remote sensing. With a background in agriculture and graduate training in environmental science and GIS, I am interested in understanding agricultural landscapes through spatial data, UAV imagery, satellite observations, and computational analysis.

My current research focuses on applying geospatial technologies to agricultural problems, with particular interest in precision agriculture, UAV-based analysis, spatial modeling, and remote sensing.

I enjoy working across the full research workflow—from collecting and processing spatial data to analyzing patterns, building reproducible workflows, and communicating results through maps and visualizations.

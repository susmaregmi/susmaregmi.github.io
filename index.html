About Me

Welcome to my academic portfolio. I am a graduate researcher passionate about leveraging geospatial technologies to solve real-world problems.

My work focuses on the intersection of GIS, UAV remote sensing, and precision agriculture. I am particularly interested in how spatial analysis and remote sensing data can be used to improve agricultural practices and natural resource management.

Research Interests

🌾 Precision Agriculture

Applying geospatial data to optimize crop management and yield.

🛰️ UAV Remote Sensing

Utilizing drones for high-resolution spatial data collection and analysis.

🗺️ GIS & Spatial Analysis

Advanced mapping and spatial modeling for environmental applications.

🌲 Tree Detection & Mapping

Automated identification and mapping of tree species using imagery.

🌍 Remote Sensing

Satellite and aerial image analysis for land cover and change detection.
